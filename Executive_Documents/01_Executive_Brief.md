## THE RATIONALE: WHY NOW?

### Market Context

1. **Industry 4.0 Imperative:** Global cement manufacturers are deploying IoT, AI, and integrated ERP systems. The competitive gap widens daily for those who delay.

2. **Nigerian Market Dynamics:** With Dangote's dominance and emerging competition, operational efficiency and dealer relationships are the primary differentiators. Digital infrastructure enables both.

3. **Regulatory Environment:** The Nigeria Data Protection Regulation (NDPR) and emerging SEC requirements for listed entities mandate robust data governance. RESIDENT CONNECT 360™ ensures compliance from day one.

4. **Investor Expectations:** Modern institutional investors demand real-time visibility and digital governance. The Investor Portal and Board Chairman Dashboard directly address this.
