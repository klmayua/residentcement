## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

This document outlines the complete 75-day implementation plan for the RESIDENT CONNECT 360™ digital ecosystem. The project is structured into two distinct phases:

- **Phase 1 (Days 1-30): Foundation Deployment** — All revenue-ready systems go live
- **Phase 2 (Days 31-75): Intelligence Layer** — Advanced operational and governance modules deployed

The approach ensures rapid value delivery while maintaining quality and minimizing business disruption.

## RISK MANAGEMENT

### Risk Register

| Risk ID | Risk | Probability | Impact | Mitigation | Owner |
|---------|------|-------------|--------|------------|-------|
| R01 | User adoption resistance | Medium | High | Change management, training, champions | PM |
| R02 | Data migration errors | Medium | High | Validation scripts, parallel run, rollback plan | BA |
| R03 | Integration complexity | Medium | Medium | API-first design, phased integration, testing | Tech Lead |
| R04 | IoT sensor failure | Low | High | Redundant sensors, local storage, alerts | IoT Engineer |
| R05 | Security breach | Low | Critical | Security testing, monitoring, incident response | DevOps |
| R06 | Scope creep | Medium | Medium | Change control process, scope freeze | PM |
| R07 | Resource unavailability | Low | High | Cross-training, backup resources | PM |
| R08 | Network connectivity (factory) | Medium | High | Offline capability, local edge processing | IoT Engineer |

### Contingency Planning

| Scenario | Response |
|----------|----------|
| **Critical bug in production** | Rollback to last stable version within 1 hour |
| **Data corruption** | Restore from last backup, replay transactions |
| **Key resource unavailable** | Activate backup resource, adjust timeline |
| **Scope change request** | Change control board review, impact assessment |
