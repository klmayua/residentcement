The Nigerian and African cement industry is at an inflection point. Urbanisation, infrastructure spending, and a growing population continue to drive demand, but the market is maturing. Dangote, BUA, and Lafarge are all investing in digital capabilities—launching e‑commerce platforms, loyalty apps, and fintech integrations—that threaten to lock in our distributors and block makers.

- **High DSO** (\<\<SIMULATED: 45 days\>\>) tying up working capital.

- **Increase revenue** by 15% through market share gains and new marketplace revenue.

- **Reduce DSO** from 45 to 30 days, freeing up significant working capital.

This transformation is not just about technology—it’s about securing our competitive future.

| Principle | Definition | Rationale | Operational Translation | Measurable Indicator |

### 11.7 Vendor Selection Criteria and Process

**Evaluation Criteria (weighted):**

| Category | Weight | Sub‑Criteria |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| Technical Fit | 35% | Alignment with architecture (API‑first, cloud‑native), scalability, security features, integration capabilities |
| Commercial | 25% | Total cost of ownership (license, implementation, support), payment terms, volume discounts |
| Support & Local Presence | 20% | Local team, response time SLAs, training offerings, references in region |
| Vendor Stability | 10% | Financial health, market reputation, roadmap alignment |
| Cultural Fit | 10% | Willingness to partner, agile methodology, references from similar clients |

**RFP Process:**

1. **Pre‑qualification:** Issue RFI to 5–7 vendors; select 3–4 for RFP.  
2. **RFP Release:** Detailed requirements, timeline, evaluation criteria.  
3. **Bidders Conference:** Virtual Q\&A.  
4. **Proposal Submission:** Technical and commercial separate.  
5. **Evaluation:** Technical team scores technical; commercial team scores commercial; weighted total.  
6. **Demonstrations:** Top 2 vendors present live demo of key scenarios.  
7. **Reference Checks:** Speak with 3 existing clients.  
8. **Negotiation:** Finalise contract terms.  
9. **Decision:** Program Board approval; Steering Committee if \>1M USD.

**Timeline:** 6–8 weeks per major procurement.

**Owner:** CTO for technical; CFO for commercial.
